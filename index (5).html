01 / How It Works

Concept to shelf-ready.

Every project moves through the same four phases — from early research through to a manufacturing-ready part and the visuals to sell it.

01

Research & Concept

  • Research the product / problem
  • Conceptualize the design direction
02

Engineer & Document

  • Model in SolidWorks
  • Reverse engineer existing components
  • Produce technical drawings
03

Prepare for Manufacture

  • Design for laser cutting / fabrication
  • Prepare files for manufacturing
04

Visualize & Market

  • Render in Blender
  • Animate the product
  • Produce the advertisement

Sheet 01 — Featured

Asado Grill

Full pipeline, start to finish. The concept began as a reference image found online, developed into a complete dimensioned assembly in SolidWorks — round grill body, removable coal basket, tilting grate, and a two-wheel cart chassis — then previewed in Blender before going to fabrication.

Fabricated by Kifaru Steel Fabricators Ltd. The finished piece below is the actual built unit, matching the model.
